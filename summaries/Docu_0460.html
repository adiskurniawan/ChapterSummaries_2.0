6. Q: What is the difference between experiencing an emotion and allowing it to control behaviour?
A: Show answer
Experiencing an emotion is the internal response to a situation, thought, memory, or bodily state. Allowing it to control behaviour means responding automatically without considering the facts, available choices, or likely consequences. A person can feel anger while choosing to ask a clarifying question, or feel fear while following a practiced safety procedure. The aim is not to eliminate emotion, because emotions provide information about needs, threats, relationships, and goals. Instead, emotional intelligence involves noticing the feeling, interpreting it cautiously, and selecting behaviour that serves a considered purpose. This distinction helps avoid the false choice between acting on every feeling and suppressing every feeling.
