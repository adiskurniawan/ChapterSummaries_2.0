5. Q: How does a transformer-based language model work at a high level?
A: Show answer
At a high level, a transformer processes text as tokens and uses attention mechanisms to represent relationships among parts of the input. During generation, the model estimates which token is likely to follow the tokens already present, then continues the sequence according to its generation process and configuration. This simplified explanation helps users understand why prompt wording and surrounding conversation can affect an answer. It is not a complete account of training, architecture, safety tuning, or inference. The chapter identifies the transformer as the neural-network architecture behind GPT but does not provide engineering specifications. Users generally do not need the mathematics to use the interface, but should understand that outputs are model-generated rather than human thoughts.

14. Q: What is the significance of Alan Turing's work to discussions of artificial intelligence?
A: Show answer
The chapter highlights Turing's work on universal computation and the Turing test. The Universal Turing Machine is a theoretical model showing how a general-purpose machine can carry out different computations when supplied with appropriate instructions and data. The imitation-game idea, later widely called the Turing test, frames whether a machine's conversational behaviour can be distinguished from a human's under specified conditions. It is historically influential, but passing a conversational test would not establish that a system understands the world, has consciousness, or is reliable in every task. The test concerns observable behaviour in a particular setting. This distinction matters when evaluating ChatGPT's human-like language.
