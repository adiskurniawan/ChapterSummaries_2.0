INGO Finance & Accounting SOP Register
1. Financial Governance and Accounting Controls
• SOP_001_Financial_Accounting_Policies_and_Chart_of_Accounts - Accounting policies, chart of accounts, accounting basis, fund codes, project codes, donor codes, and cost centres
• SOP_002_Budget_Preparation_Approval_Revision_and_Monitoring - Annual budgets, project budgets, budget amendments, commitment controls, and budget-versus-actual reporting
• SOP_003_Procurement_and_Supplier_Management - Supplier onboarding, due diligence, purchase approvals, quotations, purchase orders, receipt of goods/services, and conflict-of-interest controls
• SOP_004_Journal_Entry_Preparation_Review_and_Posting - Manual and recurring journals, accruals, adjustments, corrections, supporting evidence, and independent approval
• SOP_005_Month_End_and_Year_End_Financial_Closing - Closing calendar, cut-off, accruals, prepayments, deferred balances, depreciation, foreign exchange revaluation, and closing review
• SOP_006_Balance_Sheet_Reconciliation - Monthly reconciliation of balance sheet accounts, supporting schedules, reconciling items, aging, and resolution deadlines
• SOP_007_General_Ledger_Suspense_and_Clearing_Accounts - General ledger integrity, suspense account investigations, clearing entries, correcting entries, and control-account restrictions
• SOP_008_Interfund_Interproject_and_Interoffice_Reconciliation - Reconciliation of transactions between projects, grants, field offices, headquarters, and related entities
• SOP_009_Financial_Statement_Preparation_and_Disclosure - Statement of financial position, financial performance, cash flows, notes, accounting policies, estimates, and required disclosures
• SOP_010_Financial_Records_Management_and_Document_Retention - Transaction documentation, audit trails, record retention, access controls, and retrieval of accounting evidence
2. Grant, Donation and Project Accounting
• SOP_011_Donor_Agreement_Review_and_Grant_Setup - Agreement review, funding restrictions, conditions, deliverables, eligible expenditure, reporting deadlines, grant codes, and approved budgets
• SOP_012_Receipt_of_Grantor_Funds_and_Donations - Receipt verification, bank deposits, remittance advice, donor identification, grant allocation, and unidentified receipts
• SOP_013_Grant_Income_Recognition - Revenue recognition and measurement based on applicable accounting requirements and the substance and terms of funding arrangements
• SOP_014_Restricted_Funds_Grant_Advances_and_Unspent_Balances - Distinguishing restrictions from conditions, assessing revenue and liabilities, tracking unspent balances, refunds, and repayment obligations
• SOP_015_Donor_Receivables_Grant_Billing_and_Cost_Recovery - Grant instalments due, reimbursement claims, receivable aging, follow-up, disputed claims, and recoverability assessment
• SOP_016_Project_Expenditure_Coding_and_Cost_Allocation - Direct and indirect cost allocation, shared costs, staff-cost allocation, overhead recovery, budget eligibility, and donor-specific coding
• SOP_017_Implementing_Partner_and_Subgrant_Management - Partner assessment, funding agreements, disbursements, liquidation, expenditure verification, monitoring, reporting, and recovery of ineligible expenditure
• SOP_018_Donor_Financial_Reporting_and_Grant_Closure - Financial reports, budget reconciliations, expenditure eligibility, final claims, outstanding advances, asset disposition, unspent funds, and grant closeout
• SOP_019_In_Kind_Donations_and_Donated_Assets - Identification, valuation where applicable, recognition, documentation, custody, use restrictions, and disclosure of non-cash contributions
3. Expenditure, Advances and Cash Management
• SOP_020_Accounts_Payable_Supplier_Invoices_and_Accrued_Liabilities - Invoice validation, purchase order and receipt matching, duplicate-invoice prevention, payment terms, accruals, and liability cut-off
• SOP_021_Payment_Processing_Approval_and_Bank_Disbursement - Payment batches, supporting documents, delegated authority, segregation of duties, bank-signatory approval, and proof of payment
• SOP_022_Reimbursement_Claim - Claim submission, receipt verification, policy eligibility, approval, accounting coding, applicable tax treatment, and reimbursement
• SOP_023_Cash_Advance_for_Project_Agreement_PKS - Advance authorization, project and agreement linkage, disbursement, expenditure evidence, liquidation, overdue advances, and recovery of unspent balances
• SOP_024_Staff_Travel_Advance_Per_Diem_and_Travel_Expense_Settlement - Travel authorization, advances, allowable expenses, per diem calculations, supporting documents, settlement deadlines, and recovery of excess advances
• SOP_025_Cash_Imprest_and_Field_Site_Cash_Advance - Imprest limits, custodianship, disbursements, replenishment, surprise cash counts, supporting documents, handover, and reconciliation
• SOP_026_Corporate_Credit_Card - Cardholder authorization, permitted expenditure, transaction limits, statement reconciliation, receipts, prohibited uses, and card cancellation
• SOP_027_Cash_and_Bank_Management - Bank-account opening and closure, authorized signatories, cash forecasting, liquidity, cash custody, deposits, transfers, and safeguarding of funds
• SOP_028_Bank_Reconciliation - Monthly bank-to-ledger reconciliation, outstanding payments, deposits in transit, bank charges, unidentified transactions, stale items, and independent review
4. Payroll, Employee Benefits and Tax Compliance
• SOP_029_Payroll_Management - Approved personnel changes, payroll calculations, salary payments, payroll journals, payroll register review, and reconciliation to the general ledger
• SOP_030_Employee_Benefits_and_Staff_Related_Accruals - Leave balances, termination benefits, applicable pension obligations, other employee benefits, and year-end accruals
• SOP_031_Tax_Withholding_VAT_and_Statutory_Tax_Transactions - Applicable tax determination, withholding, tax invoices, recoverable or payable VAT where applicable, tax evidence, and reconciliation to tax accounts
• SOP_032_Monthly_Income_Tax_Filing - Preparation, review, payment coordination, submission, evidence retention, and reconciliation of applicable periodic tax returns
• SOP_033_Annual_Income_Tax_Filing - Annual tax computation, accounting-to-tax reconciliation, supporting schedules, review, submission, and filing evidence retention
5. Foreign Exchange, Assets and Financial Instruments
• SOP_034_Realized_and_Unrealized_Foreign_Exchange_Gain_Loss - Exchange-rate sources, transaction-date rates, reporting-date revaluation, realized versus unrealized gains/losses, journal entries, and reconciliation
• SOP_035_Investments_Deposits_and_Treasury_Income - Investment approval and custody, fixed deposits, interest income, applicable valuation, maturity monitoring, and financial reporting
• SOP_036_Property_Plant_and_Equipment_and_Capital_Expenditure - Capitalization criteria, asset register, tagging, custody, depreciation, impairment, physical verification, transfers, and disposal
• SOP_037_Intangible_Assets_and_Software - Recognition, capitalization, useful lives, amortization, impairment, software implementation costs, and disposal or retirement
• SOP_038_Inventory_Supplies_and_Consumables - Stock receipt, inventory valuation, issuance, physical counts, damage or obsolescence, write-offs, and accounting reconciliation
• SOP_039_Lease_Accounting - Identification of lease arrangements, initial recognition, subsequent measurement, lease payments, modifications, reassessment, and disclosures under the applicable framework
• SOP_040_Receivables_Financial_Assets_and_Impairment - Classification and measurement of financial assets, staff and partner receivables, recoverability, applicable expected credit losses, and write-offs
• SOP_041_Financial_Liabilities_Loans_and_Borrowings - Recognition and measurement of liabilities, contractual obligations, repayment schedules, interest, and disclosures
• SOP_042_Provisions_Contingent_Liabilities_and_Contingent_Assets - Legal claims, disputed obligations, applicable onerous contracts, provision estimates, contingent items, and year-end review
6. Audit, Assurance and Accountability
• SOP_043_Year_End_and_Statutory_Audit_Preparation - Audit timetable, trial balance, reconciliations, supporting schedules, financial statements, confirmations, audit evidence, and information requests
• SOP_044_Donor_Audit_and_Audit_Finding_Follow_Up - Donor-specific audit requirements, sample preparation, findings register, management responses, corrective actions, deadlines, and closure verification
• SOP_045_Fraud_Suspected_Misconduct_and_Irregular_Expenditure - Reporting channels, escalation, evidence preservation, investigation responsibilities, approval restrictions, recovery, and referral under organizational policy
• SOP_046_Financial_Controls_Delegation_of_Authority_and_Segregation_of_Duties - Approval limits, incompatible duties, system access, independent checks, temporary delegations, periodic access reviews, and exception reporting
• SOP_047_Financial_Compliance_Calendar_and_Obligation_Monitoring - Central register of tax deadlines, statutory filings, donor reports, grant milestones, insurance renewals, audit deliverables, and responsible officers
