2. Q: Why are initials such as RA not enough to identify someone reliably?
A: Show answer
Two or more employees can share the same initials, especially in a large organization. “RA” could represent many combinations of first and last names, and some systems may display preferred names, middle initials, shortened names, or usernames that make the situation even less obvious. A reliable search therefore combines the initials with other evidence such as department, office, job title, country, project, supervisor, or email domain. The objective is not simply to find a person whose name starts with those letters but to identify the correct person in the relevant organizational context.

Study instruction:
- “Understand the difference between finding a matching string and identifying the correct person.”

Key point:
- Initials are an identifier clue, not proof of identity.
- Context is necessary to distinguish among multiple possible matches.
